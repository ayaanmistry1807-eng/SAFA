Who We Are

Where Passion
Meets Purpose

Saeed Ahmed Football Academy (SAFA) is Mumbra's premier football development hub, offering world-class, structured training for aspiring players aged 4 years and above.

We believe every child — regardless of gender or background — deserves the opportunity to learn the beautiful game. Our sessions are free and fair for both girls and boys.

  • AIFF D Licensed Head Coach
  • Age-appropriate training modules
  • Equal opportunity for girls & boys
  • Focus on skill, fitness & teamwork
